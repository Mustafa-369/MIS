Organisation & Authority

The people structure of the Aaliqadr Operations Platform

v1.0 · aligned to BRAIN v1.1 Companion to THE BRAIN Jeedimetla · Ranigunj Position drives roles

This document defines the seats of the organisation — who reports to whom, and what each seat is allowed to do. It names positions, not people: individuals are placed into these seats in the software, and one person may hold several. Built on the BRAIN's rule that a position drives its permissions, it is the source for Wave 1.

2 · What links to what

The links

  • A department contains many positions; one is its head.
  • A position is held by employees; an employee may hold several (many-to-many).
  • A position drives roles (also many-to-many) — a person's access is the combined roles of every seat they hold.
  • A position reports to another position — the chain the approval engine reads.

6 · Authority rules

  • Order release. Primary authority = General Manager; backup = Head of Production & Planning when the GM is away. A configured approval right, not a role.
  • Quality is independent. The Head of Quality & Engineering reports to the General Manager / Board, never into Production — so output pressure cannot overrule quality.
  • Segregation of duties. Buying (procurement), receiving (stores), and paying (finance) are distinct roles held by distinct seats wherever staffing allows.
  • Multi-position. A person's permissions are the union of the roles of every seat they hold. Several top seats (General Manager, System Administrator, Procurement) may be held together today; they are named separately so they can be delegated as the business grows.

⚑ Critical-part QC authorisation — build this into the UI

Because the Head of Quality & Engineering holds both quality and design, a part they designed could otherwise be quality-signed by the same person. For parts marked critical / important, the quality gate requires an extra authorisation from a Director / General Manager, on top of the Quality Head. This is a three-part requirement to carry into the build:

  • a critical flag on the item register;
  • an approval rule: critical part + quality gate → mandatory Director sign-off (a hard gate);
  • surfaced on the Quality-gate screen as a required second authorisation before the lot can pass.

Logged here so it is not lost when the Quality module (Wave 5) is built.
